Definitions & Formula
Billability % = Billable Resources Total Billed + VAR + Floater + Bench × 100
Metric Definitions
Billed
Assigned to client projects and billed accordingly.
Floater
Temporary backup covering for billed resources during leave or transitions.
Training
Ramp-up resources - anyone on an account over 3 months moves to Floater instead.
VAR (Value-Added Resource)
Leadership/support capacity, capped around 21% - roughly 2 Leads + 1 Sr. Lead per 14 employees.
Bench
Currently unassigned to any account.
None
On notice period or maternity leave.
